Our Story

Three Generations of Family Recipes

Recipe Secrets started in 1987 when Maria Russo began selling her mother's recipes out of a small kitchen window in Maple Town. Today, her children and grandchildren run the dining room you see now — but every dish still starts with a recipe card from the family box.

1987

Maria opens a small takeout window serving her mother's braised short rib and tiramisu.

2004

The family moves into the current dining room on Harvest Lane, seating 60 guests.

Today

Three generations of the Russo family still cook, serve, and greet guests every night.
